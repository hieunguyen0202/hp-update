Words: 36 Lessons: Clothes and Fashion

I’d like to talk about advanced clothes and fashion. An apron/ˈeɪprən/, badge/bæʤ/, bathrobe/ˈbæθˌroʊb/, bikini/bɪˈkiːni/, cardigan/ˈkɑrdɪɡən/, helmet/ˈhɛlmɪt/, mask/mæsk/, or miniskirt/ˈmɪniˌskɝːt/ changes the outfit/ˈaʊtˌfɪt/ mood. A jersey/ˈʤɝːzi/, sandal/ˈsændəl/, vest/vɛst/, hood/hʊd/, and zipper/ˈzɪpɚ/ sit next to fabric/ˈfæbrɪk/ like denim/ˈdɛnɪm/, lace/leɪs/, lining/ˈlaɪnɪŋ/, silk/sɪlk/, or wooly/ˈwʊli/ knit. A designer/dɪˈzaɪnɚ/ collection/kəˈlɛkʃən/ for modeling/ˈmɑdlɪŋ/ can look glamorous/ˈɡlæmərəs/, stylish/ˈstaɪlɪʃ/, sporty/ˈspɔrti/, striped/straɪpt/, plain/pleɪn/, casual/ˈkæʒuəl/, or matching/ˈmæʧɪŋ/. My wardrobe/ˈwɔrdˌroʊb/ helps me dress up/drɛs ʌp/ for a masquerade/ˌmæskəˈreɪd/ or stay undressed/ʌnˈdrɛst/ comfort at home after work. When colors match/mætʃ/, the whole look feels complete — that’s my B2 speaking blog on clothes. Tôi muốn nói về clothes và fashion nâng cao. Apron, badge, bathrobe, bikini, cardigan, helmet, mask, hoặc miniskirt đổi mood outfit. Jersey, sandal, vest, hood, zipper cạnh fabric như denim, lace, lining, silk, hoặc wooly. Collection của designer cho modeling có thể glamorous, stylish, sporty, striped, plain, casual, hoặc matching. Wardrobe giúp dress up cho masquerade hoặc undressed thoải mái ở nhà. Khi màu match, tổng thể trọn — đó là blog speaking B2 về clothes. Costume cũng vẫn là từ hay dùng.

And one more word I keep using when I talk about clothes and fashion is costume/ˈkɑstuːm/ , /ˈkɑstjum/. Và thêm một từ tôi hay dùng khi nói về clothes and fashion là costume.
