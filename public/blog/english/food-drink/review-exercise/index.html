2 · Mental model — Tenses for Food speaking

Gom 6 nhóm thì thành 3 nhánh thời gian — chọn một nhánh cho mỗi câu trả lời.

Food & habits · Tense map
Present
  • Simple — facts, habits, preferences
    I usually have rice. / Fast food is convenient.
  • Continuous — now, changing trends
    I'm trying a low-carb diet. / People are eating more plant-based food.
  • Perfect — experience until now
    I've tried sushi. / I've always liked spicy food.
  • Perfect Continuous — duration until now
    I've been cooking at home more lately.
Past
  • Simple — finished events
    Last Sunday I grilled kebab in the garden.
  • Continuous — background action
    I was chopping vegetables when my friend arrived.
  • Perfect — earlier past
    I had never tried lobster before that trip.
  • used to / would — past habits
    I would eat fast food every day as a teenager.
Future
  • will / going to — plans & predictions
    I'm going to cook pasta tonight. / People will eat healthier.
  • Future Perfect — completed by a deadline
    By next year I will have tried ten new cuisines.
  • be about to — immediate future
    I'm about to order takeout.

Linear Thinking rule: pick one time frame per sentence — don't mix tenses without a clear link (when / before / after).

    • Earlier past — event A happened before event B (both in the past)
    • Never before — I had never tried lobster before that trip.
    • Timeline — had tried → then visited → then tasted

    Food: Before that dinner, I had never tasted blue cheese — so the flavour shocked me.

  • Tell a story (past tense) Watch video ↗ Part 2 meal narrative — 4-step structure

    • 1 · Background — who / when / where / what — set the scene
    • 2 · Goal — what did people want? — We had to finish cooking before guests arrived.
    • 3 · Tension — problems, difficulties, foreshadowing
    • 4 · Ending — resolve tension + retrospective comment — Looking back, it was stressful but fun.

  • Talk about the future Watch video ↗ Diet plans, food trends, predictions
    Notes & mental model (after watching)

    • going to / planning to — intention, not fully fixed — I'm planning to try a low-carb diet.
    • Present Simple — timetables — The cooking class starts at 11:30.
    • will / won't — predictions — People will eat more plant-based food.
    • may / might — uncertainty — I might order takeout tonight.

4 · Core formulas — Lesson 2 & 3

Công thức IELTS Nguyễn Huyền — Lesson 3 (Yes/No + Reasons) ghép Lesson 2 (lý do thích / không thích). Chọn 1–2 nhánh, không nhồi hết.

Lesson 3 · Do you like X?

Công thức: Yes/No + Reasons (dùng Lesson 2)

Do you like X?
├── YES
│   ├── Yes, definitely / absolutely
│   ├── I + V (like / love / enjoy) + V-ing
│   ├── I'm + adj (interested in / keen on)
│   └── I'm a + NP (big fan of)
├── NO
│   ├── No, definitely / absolutely not · No, not really
│   ├── I + DON'T + V
│   ├── I'm + NOT + adj
│   └── I'm NOT a + NP
└── REASONS
    ├── Because / This is because + S + V
    └── Because of + noun / noun phrase
YesNo
DirectYes, definitely / absolutelyNo, definitely not · No, not really
VerbI like / love / enjoy + V-ingI don't like / love / enjoy
AdjI'm keen on / interested inI'm not keen on / interested in
NPI'm a big fan ofI'm not a big fan of
Reasonbecause / This is because + S + V · because of + noun/NP
  • It gives me the chance to + V
  • I also get the opportunity to + V
  • It's a great way to + V · It also helps me + V
  • can lead to various health problems, such as…

Lưu ý: because it is not good for my health (mệnh đề) ↔ because of its harmful effects on my health (cụm danh từ)

Thực hành · Food & general

Do you like cooking?

Yes, definitely. I'm keen on cooking because it gives me the chance to try new recipes and unwind after work.

Vâng, chắc chắn. Tôi thích nấu ăn vì nó cho tôi cơ hội thử món mới và thư giãn sau giờ làm.

Do you like fast food?

No, definitely not because it's not good for my health. Consuming too much fast food can lead to various health problems, such as diabetes, high blood pressure or even cancer.

Không, chắc chắn không vì không tốt cho sức khỏe. Ăn quá nhiều đồ ăn nhanh có thể dẫn đến tiểu đường, cao huyết áp hoặc ung thư.

Do you like trying new cuisines?

Yes, absolutely. I'm a big fan of food from different cultures. This is because it gives me the chance to explore different traditions and widen my horizons.

Có, tôi là fan của ẩm thực đa dạng — được khám phá truyền thống và mở rộng tầm nhìn.

Do you like playing sports? health

Yes, definitely, because it's a great way to keep fit and stay healthy. It also helps me strengthen my muscles and burn excess calories.

Vâng — giữ dáng, khỏe mạnh, tăng cơ và đốt calo.

Do you like music? V-ing subject

Yes, absolutely. I'm a big fan of music. This is because listening to music helps me relax, unwind and temporarily forget all the pressures from my work.

Nghe nhạc giúp thư giãn và tạm quên áp lực công việc. (listening to music = V-ing làm chủ ngữ)

Do you like your job? soft no

Well, not really because my job is quite boring. It doesn't give me the chance to try anything new. I have to deal with the same tasks every day.

Không thực sự thích — công việc nhàm, không có gì mới, lặp lại mỗi ngày.

Grammar notes (Lesson 3)
  • V-ing làm chủ ngữ: Listening to music helps me relax. · Watching movies is a great way to unwind.
  • -ing vs -ed: boring (tính chất) vs bored (cảm xúc) — This movie is boring · It makes me bored
  • every day (adv) vs everyday (adj)

Lesson 2 · Reasons like / dislike

Hai trụ: mang tính giải trí · mang tính giáo dục (+ sức khỏe)

BẢNG LÝ DO THÍCH & KHÔNG THÍCH
├── LÝ DO THÍCH
│   ├── Giải trí → relax / unwind / recharge / escape…
│   ├── Giáo dục → learn skills / widen horizons / enrich knowledge
│   └── Sức khỏe → keep fit / prevent health problems
└── LÝ DO KHÔNG THÍCH
    ├── Không giải trí → boring / stressful / makes me tired
    └── Không giáo dục / hại SK → doesn't help · can lead to obesity, cancer…

3 · LÝ DO THÍCH — Mang tính giải trí

It's + adj (interesting / entertaining / exciting / relaxing …)

reduce stressrelax / unwindclear my head recharge my batteriesescape from reality escape from the hustle and bustle of the city temporarily forget pressures from workbeing in nature

Why do people like home-cooked meals?

I think because it's a great way to unwind and recharge their batteries — especially when they're tired after work.

It's + adj (educational / useful / practical …)

learn various skills such as + N/NP ↔ learn how to + V

Skilllearn how to…

Do you like reading about food & nutrition?

Yes, because it helps me learn how to manage my diet better and make healthier choices.

5 · LÝ DO THÍCH — Sức khỏe
keep fit / stay healthyimprove my health strengthen my musclesburn excess calories maintain a healthy weight prevent high blood pressure / stroke / heart attack / cancer

Do you like eating vegetables?

Yes, because it's a great way to stay healthy and prevent various health problems.

7 · LÝ DO KHÔNG THÍCH — Mang tính giải trí
  1. It's + not + adj (not interesting / relaxing …)
  2. It's + adj tiêu cực (boring / terrible / stressful / noisy)
  3. It makes me + adj (stressed / exhausted / bored)
  4. I have to + … (deal with the same tasks every day)

not my cup of tea · can't stand · I can't bear

8 · LÝ DO KHÔNG THÍCH — Mang tính giáo dục

It's + not + adj (educational / useful / practical)

Rule: doesn't + V nguyên mẫu — It doesn't help me relax · It doesn't give me the chance to…

Do you like repetitive kitchen work?

No, because it doesn't give me the chance to learn anything new — I have to do the same tasks every day, which makes me bored.

9 · LÝ DO KHÔNG THÍCH — Sức khỏe

It's + unhealthy / not good for / harmful to / detrimental to + your health

… can lead to diabetes, high blood pressure, heart attack, cancer

Do you like fast food?

No, because it's not good for my health. Consuming too much can lead to obesity and heart problems.
