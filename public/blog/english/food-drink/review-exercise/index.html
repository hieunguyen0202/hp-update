1 · Grammar foundations

Đọc lý thuyết cơ bản trước khi luyện nói. Mỗi thì gắn với ngữ cảnh Food & Drink.

  • Present Simple & Present Continuous

    Thói quen ăn uống, sở thích, xu hướng hiện tại

    e.g. I usually have… / I'm trying a low-carb diet

  • Past Simple & Past Continuous · used to · would

    Kỷ niệm ẩm thực, món từng thích, đang nấu thì…

    e.g. When I was a child, I would… / I was cooking when…

  • Future Simple & near future

    Kế hoạch nấu ăn, dự đoán thói quen ăn

    e.g. I'm going to cook… / Food will become…

  • Future Perfect · Continuous · be about to

    Dự đoán dài hạn về ẩm thực

    e.g. By next year I will have tried…

  • Past Perfect & Past Perfect Continuous

    Kể chuyện bữa ăn (trước / trong khi / sau)

    e.g. I had never tried… before I visited…

  • Present Perfect & Present Perfect Continuous

    Kinh nghiệm ẩm thực đến nay

    e.g. I've always liked… / I've been eating more…

2 · Mental model — Tenses for Food speaking

Gom 6 nhóm thì thành 3 nhánh thời gian — chọn một nhánh cho mỗi câu trả lời.

Food & habits · Tense map
Present
  • Simple — facts, habits, preferences
    I usually have rice. / Fast food is convenient.
  • Continuous — now, changing trends
    I'm trying a low-carb diet. / People are eating more plant-based food.
  • Perfect — experience until now
    I've tried sushi. / I've always liked spicy food.
  • Perfect Continuous — duration until now
    I've been cooking at home more lately.
Past
  • Simple — finished events
    Last Sunday I grilled kebab in the garden.
  • Continuous — background action
    I was chopping vegetables when my friend arrived.
  • Perfect — earlier past
    I had never tried lobster before that trip.
  • used to / would — past habits
    I would eat fast food every day as a teenager.
Future
  • will / going to — plans & predictions
    I'm going to cook pasta tonight. / People will eat healthier.
  • Future Perfect — completed by a deadline
    By next year I will have tried ten new cuisines.
  • be about to — immediate future
    I'm about to order takeout.

Linear Thinking rule: pick one time frame per sentence — don't mix tenses without a clear link (when / before / after).

4 · Core formulas — Lesson 2 & 3

Cấu trúc trọng tâm trước khi ghép từ mới.

Lesson 3 · Do you like X?

Yes/No + Reasons

  • Yes, definitely. / No, not really.
  • I'm keen on / I'm a big fan of + noun
  • This is because + S + V
  • It gives me the chance to + V

Food: Do you like cooking? → Yes, definitely. I'm keen on cooking because it gives me the chance to try new recipes.

Lesson 2 · Reasons

Like → entertaining or educational (+ health)

  • It's a great way to unwind / relax
  • It helps me recharge my batteries
  • Dislike → can lead to health problems
  • not my cup of tea · can't stand

Food: Why dislike fast food? → Because it can lead to obesity and it's not my cup of tea.
