Lesson 2 · Reasons like / dislike

Hai trụ: mang tính giải trí · mang tính giáo dục (+ sức khỏe)

Luồng ráp câu: ① Mở (I love this…) → because → Nhánh 1 (It's + adj) hoặc Nhánh 2 (starter + cụm V). Chọn tối đa 1–2 nhánh. Hover vào cụm tiếng Anh để xem nghĩa VI.

Vuốt ngang nếu sơ đồ rộng hơn màn hình

Không giải trí not entertaining
① Mở I don't like this · I can't stand … · It's not my cup of tea
because
Nhánh 1 · It's + adj
  • It's + not + interesting / entertaining / exciting / thrilling / relaxing
  • It's + boring / terrible / scary / difficult / stressful / noisy
Nhánh 2 · It makes me + adj

It makes me + bored / tired / stressed / exhausted · I have to + deal with the same tasks every day

  • not my cup of tea
  • can't stand
  • I can't bear
  • I have to do lots of homework
  • I have to memorise long lists of new words
  • I have to deal with difficult customers
  • I have to deal with the same tasks and the same clients every day
Không giáo dục not educational
① Mở I don't think … · To be honest, I don't enjoy …
because
Nhánh 1 · It's + not + adj
  • It's + not + educational / useful / practical
Nhánh 2 · doesn't + V

doesn't + V nguyên mẫu · It doesn't give me the chance to … · It doesn't help me learn skills such as …

  • It doesn't help me relax
  • It doesn't give me the chance to challenge myself
  • It doesn't help me learn skills such as problem-solving
  • It doesn't give me the opportunity to widen my horizons
  • It doesn't help me enrich my knowledge
  • It doesn't give me the chance to try anything new
Hại sức khỏe bad for health
① Mở No, definitely not because … · I avoid …
because
Nhánh 1 · not good / harmful
  • not good for your health
  • harmful / detrimental to your health
  • It's + unhealthy
Nhánh 2 · can lead to …

Consuming too much … can lead to …

  • diabetes
  • high blood pressure
  • stroke
  • heart attack
  • cancer
  • obesity
Reasons Dislike ↔ Like
Giải trí entertainment
① Mở I love this · I think … · I'm keen on …
because
Nhánh 1 · It's + adj
  • It's + relaxing / exciting / thrilling / entertaining / interesting …
Nhánh 2 · Starter + V

It helps me + V · It's a great way to + V · It gives me the chance to + V · I also get the opportunity to + V

  • reduce stress
  • relax / unwind
  • clear my head
  • recharge my batteries
  • express my inner feelings
  • escape from reality
  • escape from the hustle and bustle of the city
  • temporarily forget all the pressures from my work
  • temporarily forget all the pressures or worries from your daily life
  • being in nature
Giáo dục educational
① Mở I love this · I think it's useful · Yes, because …
because
Nhánh 1 · It's + adj
  • It's + educational / useful / practical
  • learn skills such as … ↔ learn how to + V
Nhánh 2 · Starter + V

It helps me + V · It gives me the chance to + V · I also get the opportunity to + V

  • meet different people
  • meet people from all walks of life
  • explore different parts of the world
  • explore different cultures and traditions
  • widen my horizons
  • enrich my knowledge
  • challenge myself / push myself to the limit
  • become more confident and independent
  • become a better version of myself
  • become a more well-rounded person
  • develop my imagination and creativity
  • learn how to deal with difficult situations more effectively
  • learn how to manage my money / budgets better
  • learn how to curb stress more effectively
  • learn how to work as a team / work effectively in a team environment
  • learn how to think more independently
Sức khỏe health
① Mở I love this · Yes, because it's a great way to …
because
Nhánh 1 · It's a great way to
  • It's a great way to + keep fit / stay healthy / keep in shape
  • It's + good for your health
Nhánh 2 · Cụm V

It helps me + V · It also helps me + V · Eating … can also prevent …

  • keep fit / stay healthy / keep in shape
  • improve my health
  • strengthen my muscles
  • burn excess calories
  • maintain a healthy weight
  • prevent various health problems such as high blood pressure
  • prevent stroke / heart attack / cancer

Trái = KHÔNG THÍCH · Phải = THÍCH. Mỗi nhánh: ① Mở → because → 2 nhánh con.

Ví dụ ráp nhanh (Food)

YES Cooking + giải trí: I love cooking because it's relaxing. It helps me unwind and temporarily forget all the pressures from my work.

YES Vegetables + SK: Yes, because it's a great way to stay healthy and prevent various health problems.

NO Fast food + SK: No, definitely not because it's not good for my health. Consuming too much can lead to various health problems, such as diabetes, heart attack, high blood pressure or even cancer.

Chi tiết · Cấu trúc mở đầu (dùng chung)
  • It helps me + V
  • It's a great way to + V
  • It gives me the chance to + V
  • I also get the opportunity to + V

Lesson 3 · Do you like X?

Công thức: Yes/No + Reasons (dùng Lesson 2)

Vuốt ngang nếu sơ đồ rộng hơn màn hình

NO phủ định
① Mở No, definitely not · No, absolutely not · No, not really · Well, not really
because
Nhánh 1 · Verb
  • I don't like / love / enjoy + V-ing
  • Food: I don't like fast food / eating too much {dessert}
Nhánh 2 · Adj / NP
  • I'm not keen on …
  • I'm not interested in …
  • I'm not a big fan of …

→ because + Lesson 2 Không thích (trái)

Do you like X? No ↔ Yes + Reasons
YES khẳng định
① Mở Yes, definitely · Yes, absolutely
because
Nhánh 1 · Verb
  • I like / love / enjoy + V-ing
  • Food: I enjoy cooking / trying new {cuisine}
Nhánh 2 · Adj / NP
  • I'm keen on … · I'm interested in …
  • I'm a big fan of …
  • This is because + S + V

→ because + Lesson 2 Thích (phải)

Reasons lý do
① Mở because · This is because · because of
because
Nhánh 1 · Mệnh đề
  • because + S + V
  • This is because + S + V
  • because it is not good for my health (mệnh đề)
Nhánh 2 · Danh từ / mở rộng

because of + noun / NP · It gives me the chance to + V · It's a great way to + V · can lead to …

  • because of its harmful effects on my health
  • It gives me the chance to + V
  • I also get the opportunity to + V
  • It also helps me + V
  • can lead to various health problems

Trái = NO · Phải = YES + Reasons. Cùng luồng ① Mở → because → ghép Lesson 2.

Lưu ý: because it is not good for my health (mệnh đề) ↔ because of its harmful effects on my health (cụm danh từ)

Ví dụ Food · Thích / Không thích

Mỗi câu hỏi có 2 hướng trả lời (độ dài IELTS). Hover câu EN → nghĩa VI. Phần gạch chân / cloze dùng trong Scroll read bên dưới.

Do you like cooking?

Do you like cooking? · Thích

Thích Yes, definitely. I'm keen on cooking at home because it's relaxing. It helps me unwind and temporarily forget all the pressures from my work. It also gives me the chance to try new recipes.

Do you like cooking? · Không thích

Không thích No, because it makes me exhausted and I have to deal with the same tasks every day. It doesn't give me the chance to try anything new.

Do you like fast food?

Do you like fast food? · Thích

Thích Yes, occasionally — it's convenient when I'm busy and it helps me unwind after a long day. Grabbing a bite with friends is also a great way to escape from reality for a while.

Do you like fast food? · Không thích

Không thích No, definitely not because it's not good for my health. Consuming too much fast food can lead to various health problems, such as diabetes, heart attack, high blood pressure or even cancer.

Do you like eating vegetables?

Do you like eating vegetables? · Thích

Thích Yes, definitely, because it's a great way to stay healthy and prevent various health problems. It also helps me strengthen my muscles.

Do you like eating vegetables? · Không thích

Không thích No, not really — plain vegetables aren't my cup of tea and they don't give me richer flavours.

Do you like trying new cuisines?

Do you like trying new cuisines? · Thích

Thích Yes, absolutely. I'm a big fan of trying new cuisines because it helps me widen my horizons and enrich my knowledge. It also gives me the chance to explore different cultures and traditions.

Do you like trying new cuisines? · Không thích

Không thích Well, not really — I'm not keen on unfamiliar food because it doesn't help me relax when I eat out.

Do you like seafood?

Do you like seafood? · Thích

Thích Yes, absolutely. I enjoy eating seafood because grabbing a bite with friends is a great way to unwind. It also gives me the chance to try new dishes.

Do you like seafood? · Không thích

Không thích No, not really — seafood isn't my cup of tea and I'm worried it can lead to allergies.

Grammar notes (Lesson 3)
  • V-ing làm chủ ngữ: Listening to music helps me relax. · Watching movies is a great way to unwind.
  • -ing vs -ed: boring (tính chất) vs bored (cảm xúc) — This movie is boring · It makes me bored
  • every day (adv) vs everyday (adj)
