Words: 52 Lessons: Home Appliances and Furniture · Houses and Buildings

Yes, definitely. I’m a big fan of describing houses and buildings. Some people dream of a palace/ˈpæləs/; I prefer a cabin/ˈkæbɪn/, a studio/ˈstuːdiˌoʊ/, a guest house/ˈɡɛst ˌhaʊs/, a country house/ˈkʌntri ˌhaʊs/, or a simple apartment building/əˈpɑrtmənt ˈbɪldɪŋ/. A resident/ˈrɛzɪdənt/ needs good accommodations/əˌkɑməˈdeɪʃənz/; rent/rɛnt/ is paid every month — yes, rent/rɛnt/ again if you share. Inside: floor/flɔːr/, step/stɛp/, fireplace/ˈfaɪɚˌpleɪs/, chimney/ˈʧɪmni/, driveway/ˈdraɪvˌweɪ/, corridor/ˈkɔrɪˌdɔr/, drain/dreɪn/, walkway/ˈwɔˌkweɪ/, back door/ˈbæk ˌdɔr/, front door/ˈfrʌnt ˌdɔr/, family room/ˈfæmɪli ˈrum/, guest room/ˈɡɛst ˌrum/, storeroom/ˈstɔrˌruːm/, study/ˈstʌdi/, shelf/ʃɛlf/, porch/pɔrtʃ/, and maybe a swimming pool/ˈswɪmɪŋ ˌpul/. Chắc chắn rồi. Tôi thích mô tả houses và buildings. Có người mơ palace; tôi thích cabin, studio, guest house, country house, hoặc apartment building đơn giản. Resident cần accommodations tốt; rent trả mỗi tháng — đúng, rent lần nữa nếu share. Bên trong: floor, step, fireplace, chimney, driveway, corridor, drain, walkway, back door, front door, family room, guest room, storeroom, study, shelf, porch, và có thể swimming pool.
