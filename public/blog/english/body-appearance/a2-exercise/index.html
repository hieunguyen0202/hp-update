A2 Exercise · Body & Appearance

Words: 96 Lessons: Appearance · Personality and Behavior · The Human Body

I prefer kind/kaɪnd/ descriptions of appearance/əˈpɪrəns/ and personality/ˌpɝsəˈnælɪti/ to rude/rud/ jokes. Some people look/lʊk/ attractive/əˈtræktɪv/, good-looking/ˈɡʊdˌlʊkɪŋ/, handsome/ˈhænsəm/, pretty/ˈprɪti/, or just cute/kjuːt/. Hair can be curly/ˈkɝːli/, wavy/ˈweɪvi/, or straight/streɪt/; someone blond/blɑːnd/ or bald/bɔːld/ may also wear a beard/bɪrd/ or a mustache/ˈmʌˌstæʃ/ , /məˈstæʃ/. A male/meɪl/ or female/ˈfiːmeɪl/ friend can look slim/slɪm/, skinny/ˈskɪni/, fit/fɪt/, or tiny/ˈtaɪni/ — I try to describe/dɪˈskraɪb/ with kind words, not rude ones. Tôi thích mô tả appearance và personality tử tế hơn đùa thô. Có người attractive, good-looking, handsome, pretty, hoặc cute. Hair có thể curly, wavy, hoặc straight; người blond hoặc bald cũng có thể có beard hay mustache. Friend male hoặc female có thể slim, skinny, fit, hoặc tiny — tôi cố describe tử tế, không rude.

To be honest, behavior/bɪˈheɪvjɚ/ and character/ˈkærɪktɚ/ are harder to describe/dɪˈskraɪb/ than looks/lʊk/. My personality/ˌpɝsəˈnælɪti/ can feel shy/ʃaɪ/ or talkative/ˈtɔkətɪv/, serious/ˈsɪriəs/ or funny/ˈfʌni/, interesting/ˈɪntrəstɪŋ/ or boring/ˈbɔrɪŋ/. An exciting/ɪkˈsaɪtɪŋ/ day feels wonderful/ˈwʌndɚfəl/, amazing/əˈmeɪzɪŋ/, excellent/ˈɛksələnt/, and awesome/ˈɔsəm/; a kind/kaɪnd/ person is nice/naɪs/ and great/ɡreɪt/. Sometimes life feels weird/wɪrd/, strange/streɪnʤ/, or tough/tʌf/; a unique/juˈnik/, creative/kriˈeɪtɪv/, brilliant/ˈbrɪljənt/ idea can still look crazy/ˈkreɪzi/. I want to stay confident/ˈkɑnfɪdənt/, helpful/ˈhɛlpfəl/, fair/fɛr/, and quiet/ˈkwaɪət/ when I’m unsure — not jealous/ˈʤɛləs/, foolish/ˈfulɪʃ/, or unhappy/ʌnˈhæpi/. Thành thật mà nói, behavior và character khó mô tả hơn vẻ ngoài. Personality tôi có lúc shy hoặc talkative, serious hoặc funny, interesting hoặc boring. Ngày exciting thì wonderful, amazing, excellent, awesome; người kind thì nice và great. Đôi khi weird, strange, hoặc tough; ý tưởng unique, creative, brilliant vẫn có thể crazy. Tôi muốn confident, helpful, fair, và quiet khi unsure — không jealous, foolish, hay unhappy.

About the human/ˈhjuːmən/ body: under the skin/skɪn/ there is blood/blʌd/, bone/boʊn/, and muscle/ˈmʌsəl/. My chest/tʃɛst/ rises with every breath/brɛθ/ and breathing/ˈbriːðɪŋ/ feels easy when I’m active/ˈæktɪv/ and strong/strɑŋ/, not weak/wiːk/. I can show/ʃoʊ/ my palm/pɑːm/ , /pɑlm/, forearm/ˈfɔːrˌɑːrm/, wrist/rɪst/, elbow/ˈɛlˌboʊ/, and finger/ˈfɪŋɡɚ/; my thigh/θaɪ/, heel/hiːl/, and throat/θroʊt/ work hard when I run. The skull/skʌl/ protects the brain/breɪn/; gum/ɡʌm/ and eyelash/ˈaɪˌlæʃ/ are small but useful. Physically/ˈfɪzɪkli/ I’m fine — I brush/brʌʃ/ my hair, smile/smaɪl/, look/lʊk/ friendly, and hide/haɪd/ stress when I appear/əˈpɪr/ calm. Other/ˈʌðɚ/ people may look similar/ˈsɪmɪlɚ/; each individual/ˌɪndəˈvɪdʒuəl/ still has their own/oʊn/ mild/maɪld/ or scary/ˈskɛri/ mood. A perfect/ˈpɝfɪkt/ day is when I feel normal/ˈnɔrməl/, certain/ˈsɝtən/, and ready to describe/dɪˈskraɪb/ how I appear. Về human body: dưới skin có blood, bone, và muscle. Chest nhịp theo breath và breathing dễ khi active và strong, không weak. Tôi show palm, forearm, wrist, elbow, finger; thigh, heel, throat làm việc khi chạy. Skull bảo vệ brain; gum và eyelash nhỏ nhưng hữu ích. Physically tôi ổn — brush hair, smile, look thân thiện, hide stress khi appear bình tĩnh. Other người có thể similar; mỗi individual vẫn own mild hoặc scary. Ngày perfect là khi normal, certain, và sẵn sàng describe cách mình appear. Còn fat trên body thì tôi nói nhẹ nhàng thôi.

To finish this body and appearance blog the way I’d speak in an exam, I also bring in backbone/ˈbækˌboʊn/, fat/fæt/, heart/hɑːrt/, and finally shoulder/ˈʃoʊldɚ/. Để kết thúc blog body and appearance theo cách nói trong phòng thi, tôi cũng nhắc backbone, fat, heart, và cuối cùng shoulder.
