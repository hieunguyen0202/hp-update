Looking back, the last time I described bodily actions was in a real story. I can beat/biːt/ a drum, clap/ˈkɫæp/, drag/dræɡ/ a bag, grab/ˈɡɹæb/ a bottle, or punch/pʌntʃ/ a pillow for fun — carefully. I bend/bɛnd/, bow/baʊ/, lean/ˈɫin/, or slouch/ˈsɫaʊtʃ/ when I’m tired; I kneel/ˈniɫ/, leap/ˈɫip/, tiptoe/ˈtɪpˌtoʊ/, crawl/ˈkɹɔɫ/, or lie down/lˈaɪ dˈaʊn/ at home. Eyes blink/ˈbɫɪŋk/, gaze/ˈɡeɪz/, squint/ˈskwɪnt/, stare/ˈstɛɹ/, or wink/ˈwɪŋk/; I chuckle/ˈtʃəkəɫ/, giggle/ˈɡɪɡəɫ/, or smirk/ˈsmɝk/ with friends. Sometimes I march/mɑrtʃ/, nod/ˈnɑd/, pace/ˈpeɪs/, or trip/trɪp/; kids give bunny ears in photos — books write [give] {sb} bunny ears/ɡˈɪv ˌɛsbˈiː bˈʌni ˈɪɹz/. I crouch/ˈkɹaʊtʃ/ to tie shoes and wake/ˈweɪk/ early for work. Nhìn lại, lần gần nhất tôi mô tả bodily actions là trong chuyện thật. Tôi beat trống, clap, drag túi, grab chai, hoặc punch gối vui — cẩn thận. Tôi bend, bow, lean, hoặc slouch khi mệt; kneel, leap, tiptoe, crawl, hoặc lie down ở nhà. Mắt blink, gaze, squint, stare, hoặc wink; tôi chuckle, giggle, hoặc smirk với bạn. Đôi khi march, nod, pace, hoặc trip; trẻ give bunny ears — sách ghi [give] {sb} bunny ears. Tôi crouch buộc giày và wake sớm đi làm.

Personality words matter too. I try to stay caring/ˈkɛɹɪŋ/, decent/ˈdisənt/, easy-going/ˈiːziɡˈoʊɪŋ/, energetic/ˌɛnɝˈdʒɛtɪk/, enthusiastic/ɪnˌθuziˈæstɪk/, and dynamic/daɪˈnæmɪk/. I dislike arrogant/ˈɛɹəɡənt/, dishonest/dɪˈsɑnəst/, greedy/ˈɡɹidi/, icy/ˈaɪsi/, impatient/ˌɪmˈpeɪʃənt/, nosy/ˈnoʊzi/, violent/ˈvaɪəɫənt/, /ˈvaɪɫənt/, or tight-fisted/ˈtaɪtˈfɪstɪd/ behavior. A bold/ˈboʊɫd/, lively/ˈɫaɪvɫi/, logical/ˈɫɑdʒɪkəɫ/, modest/mɑdəst/, moral/ˈmɔɹəɫ/, optimistic/ˌɑptəˈmɪstɪk/, passionate/ˈpæʃənət/, practical/ˈpræktɪkəl/, reasonable/ˈɹizənəbəɫ/, /ˈɹiznəbəɫ/, and respectable/ɹiˈspɛktəbəɫ/, /ɹɪˈspɛktəbəɫ/ attitude feels sincere/sɪnˈsɪɹ/. Self-confident/ˌsɛɫfˈkɑnfədənt/ people can still be sensitive/ˈsɛnsətɪv/, /ˈsɛnsɪtɪv/ and sympathetic/ˌsɪmpəˈθɛtɪk/; moody/ˈmudi/, pessimistic/ˌpɛsəˈmɪstɪk/, unreliable/ˌənɹiˈɫaɪəbəɫ/, /ˌənɹɪˈɫaɪəbəɫ/, unstable/ənˈsteɪbəɫ/, or unsure/ʌnˈʃʊr/ , /ʌnˈʃɔːr/ days happen. Strict/ˈstɹɪkt/ or lenient/ˈɫiniənt/, /ˈɫinjənt/ teachers both shape us; tough/tʌf/ weeks need a strong-willed/stɹˈɔŋwˈɪld/ plan, not forgetful/fɝˈɡɛtfəɫ/, /fɔɹˈɡɛtfəɫ/ chaos. Từ personality cũng quan trọng. Tôi cố caring, decent, easy-going, energetic, enthusiastic, dynamic. Không thích arrogant, dishonest, greedy, icy, impatient, nosy, violent, tight-fisted. Thái độ bold, lively, logical, modest, moral, optimistic, passionate, practical, reasonable, respectable thì sincere. Người self-confident vẫn có thể sensitive và sympathetic; ngày moody, pessimistic, unreliable, unstable, unsure vẫn tới. Thầy strict hoặc lenient đều ảnh hưởng; tuần tough cần kế hoạch strong-willed, không forgetful.

Self-care is part of my routine: electric razor/ɪlˈɛktɹɪk ɹˈeɪzɚ/ or shaver/ˈʃeɪvɝ/, sunscreen/ˈsənskɹin/, /sənsˈkɹin/, shampoo/ʃæmˈpu/, conditioner/kənˈdɪʃənɝ/, balm/bɑːm/, /bɑːlm/, lotion/ˈɫoʊʃən/, and cosmetics/kɑzˈmɛtɪks/. Gel/ˈdʒɛɫ/, hairspray/hˈɛɹ spɹˈeɪ/, eyeliner/ˈaɪˌɫaɪnɝ/, eyeshadow/ˈaɪˈʃædoʊ/, blush/ˈbɫəʃ/, concealer/kənsˈiːlɚ/, face powder/fˈeɪs pˈaʊdɚ/, foundation/faʊnˈdeɪʃən/, lip gloss/lˈɪp ɡlˈɔs/, lipstick/ˈɫɪpˌstɪk/, mascara/mæˈskɛɹə/, and nail polish/nˈeɪl pˈɑːlɪʃ/ sit on the shelf. A face mask/fˈeɪs mˈæsk/, dye/ˈdaɪ/, tweezers/ˈtwizɝz/, cologne/kəˈɫoʊn/, deodorant/diˈoʊdɝənt/, mouthwash/ˈmaʊθˌwɑʃ/, dental floss/dˈɛntəl flˈɔs/, nail file/nˈeɪlfaɪl/, cotton swab/kˈɑːʔn̩ swˈɑːb/, makeup/ˈmeɪˌkəp/, and nail clippers/neɪl klɪpɚz/ finish the kit. I won’t detail every private item, but a tampon/ˈtæmpɑn/ belongs to real adult talk too. Self-care là routine: electric razor hoặc shaver, sunscreen, shampoo, conditioner, balm, lotion, cosmetics. Gel, hairspray, eyeliner, eyeshadow, blush, concealer, face powder, foundation, lip gloss, lipstick, mascara, nail polish trên kệ. Face mask, dye, tweezers, cologne, deodorant, mouthwash, dental floss, nail file, cotton swab, makeup, nail clippers hoàn thiện. Tôi không đi sâu đồ riêng tư, nhưng tampon cũng thuộc nói chuyện người lớn.

Anatomy/əˈnætəmi/ helps advanced speaking: an organ/ˈɔɹɡən/, Adam's apple/ˈædəmz ˈæpəl/, artery/ˈɑɹtɝi/, blood vessel/blˈʌd vˈɛsəl/, cell/ˈsɛɫ/, heartbeat/ˈhɑɹtˌbit/, and scalp/ˈskæɫp/. Collarbone/ˈkɑɫɝˌboʊn/, breast/brɛst/, abdomen/ˈæbdəmən/, /æbˈdoʊmən/, digestive system/daɪdʒˈɛstɪv sˈɪstəm/, immune system/ɪmjˈuːn sˈɪstəm/, belly/ˈbeli/, belly button/ˈbeliˌbʌtn/, intestine/ˌɪnˈtɛstən/, gallbladder/ˈɡɔɫˌbɫædɝ/, bladder/ˈblædər/, and buttock/ˈbətək/ are clinical but useful. Spine/ˈspaɪn/, calf/ˈkæf/, shin/ˈʃɪn/, big toe/bˈɪɡ tˈoʊ/, pinky/ˈpɪŋki/, eyelid/ˈaɪˌɫɪd/, jaw/ˈdʒɔ/, vein/ˈveɪn/, nostril/ˈnɑstɹɪɫ/, eyebrow/ˈaɪˌbɹaʊ/ — and a painful kidney stone/kˈɪdni stˈoʊn/ — complete the picture. That’s my B2 body-and-appearance blog for the exam room. Anatomy giúp speaking nâng cao: organ, Adam's apple, artery, blood vessel, cell, heartbeat, scalp.
