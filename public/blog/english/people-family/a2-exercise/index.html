Generally speaking, my friends and family come from many places. My neighbor/ˈneɪbɚ/ is Vietnamese/ˌviˌɛtˌnɑˈmiz/ from Vietnam/ˌviˌɛtˈnɑm/; another dude/duːd/ , /djuːd/ in my group/ɡruːp/ is Australian/ɔˈstreɪljən/ from Australia/ɔˈstreɪljə/. A Swiss/swɪs/ guy/ɡaɪ/ from Switzerland/ˈswɪtsɚˌlænd/ and an Austrian/ˈɔstriən/ girl from Austria/ˈɔstriə/ joined our class. Someone Dutch/dʌʧ/ from the Netherlands/ˈnɛðɚləndz/, a Norwegian/nɔrˈwiːdʒən/ from Norway/ˈnɔrˌweɪ/, and a Swedish/ˈswiːdɪʃ/ friend from Sweden/ˈswiːdən/ shared photos. A polish/ˈpoʊlɪʃ/ student from Poland/ˈpoʊlənd/ — wait, Polish — also came, plus an Egyptian/ɪˈdʒɪpʃən/ from Egypt/ˈiːdʒɪpt/ and a Turkish/ˈtɝkɪʃ/ classmate from Turkey/tˈɜːki/. Nhìn chung, bạn bè và family tôi đến từ nhiều nơi. Neighbor tôi Vietnamese từ Vietnam; một dude trong group là Australian từ Australia. Guy Swiss từ Switzerland và girl Austrian từ Austria vào lớp. Ai đó Dutch từ the Netherlands, Norwegian từ Norway, và friend Swedish từ Sweden khoe ảnh. Student Polish từ Poland cũng tới, cùng Egyptian từ Egypt và classmate Turkish từ Turkey.

We also met a Greek/ɡriːk/ tourist from Greece/ɡriːs/, a Saudi/ˈsaʊdi/ guest/ɡɛst/ from Saudi Arabia/ˈsaʊdi əˈreɪbiə/, an Afghan/ˈæfɡæn/ engineer from Afghanistan/æfˈɡænəˌstæn/, and an Israeli/ɪzˈreɪli/ doctor from Israel/ˈɪzreɪəl/. My daddy/ˈdædi/ and mommy/ˈmɑːmi/ still call my grandparent/ˈɡrændˌpɛrənt/ every Sunday — grandpa/ˈɡrændpɑː/ , /ˈɡrænpɑː/ tells stories, grandma/ˈɡrændmɑː/ , /ˈɡrænmɑː/ cooks. Their granddaughter/ˈɡrændˌdɔːtɚ/ and grandson/ˈɡrændˌsʌn/ are twins/twɪn/; each kid/kɪd/ is a cheerful member/ˈmɛmbɚ/ of the family. My middle name/ˈmɪdl ˌneɪm/ is soft; my surname/ˈsɝːˌneɪm/ — or family name/ˈfæməli ˌneɪm/ — is on every form. Chúng tôi cũng gặp tourist Greek từ Greece, guest Saudi từ Saudi Arabia, engineer Afghan từ Afghanistan, và doctor Israeli từ Israel. Daddy và mommy gọi grandparent mỗi Chủ nhật — grandpa kể chuyện, grandma nấu. Granddaughter và grandson là twin; mỗi kid là member vui của family. Middle name nhẹ; surname hay family name trên mọi form.

About relationships: a couple/ˈkʌpəl/ can marry/ˈmæri/ after they date/deɪt/ for a while, or they may break up/breɪk ʌp/ if they grow/ɡroʊ/ apart. A partner/ˈpɑːrtnɚ/ should care/kɛr/, raise/reɪz/ a kid/kɪd/ with patience, and plan a wedding/ˈwɛdɪŋ/ with family background/ˈbækˌɡraʊnd/ in mind. Honestly, inviting a guest/ɡɛst/ to a small group/ɡruːp/ dinner is how I keep friendships warm. Về quan hệ: một couple có thể marry sau khi date một thời gian, hoặc break up nếu grow xa nhau. Partner nên care, raise kid kiên nhẫn, và lên kế hoạch wedding với family background. Mời guest ăn tối trong group nhỏ là cách tôi giữ tình bạn.
