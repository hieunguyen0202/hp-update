Yes/jɛs/, definitely — greetings matter every day when I talk about people/ˈpi:pəl/ and family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/. Hello/həˈloʊ/ — or just hi/haɪ/ — is how I start every conversation, and goodbye/ɡʊdˈbaɪ/ or bye/baɪ/ is how I finish. Good morning/ɡʊd ˈmɔrnɪŋ/, good afternoon/ɡʊd ˌæftərˈnuːn/, and good evening/ɡʊd ˈiːvnɪŋ/ feel polite; good night/ɡʊd ˈnaɪt/ is for the end of the day. I always say thank you/θæŋk juː/ or thanks/θæŋks/, please/pliːz/, and sorry/ˈsɑːri/ when I need to, and welcome/ˈwɛlkəm/ when someone visits. Chắc chắn rồi — lời chào quan trọng mỗi ngày khi nói về người và gia đình. xin chào — hoặc hi — là cách tôi bắt đầu, Tạm biệt hoặc Tạm biệt! là cách kết thúc. Chào buổi sáng, chào buổi chiều, Chào buổi tối lịch sự; Chúc ngủ ngon cho cuối ngày. Tôi luôn nói cảm ơn hoặc cảm ơn, làm ơn, Xin lỗi khi cần, và Chào mừng khi có khách.

If someone asks a question/ˈkwɛsʧən/, I try to give a clear answer/ˈænsɚ/: yes/jɛs/ or no/noʊ/, or OK/ˌoʊkˈeɪ/ if I’m ready/ˈrɛdi/. My name/neɪm/ is Hieu; my last name/læst neɪm/ is Nguyen. My age/eɪdʒ/ is easy to remember, and my birthday/ˈbɝːθˌdeɪ/ — or birthdate/ˈbɝːθˌdeɪt/ — is in August. My address/ˈædˌrɛs/, /əˈdrɛs/ is in the city, my phone number/foʊn ˈnʌmbɚ/ is on my phone, and I keep my passport/ˈpæˌspɔrt/ ready when I travel. I’m still single/ˈsɪŋɡəl/, not married/ˈmæˌrid/ yet. Tôi vẫn độc thân, chưa đã kết hôn.

If I had to describe my family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/, it would have to be this. My father/ˈfɑːðɚ/ — I also say dad/dæd/ — and my mother/ˈmʌðɚ/ — mom/mɑːm/ — are my favourite parent/ˈpɛrənt/ pair. I have one sister/ˈsɪstɚ/ and one brother/ˈbrʌðɚ/. My grandmother/ˈɡrænˌmʌðɚ/ and grandfather/ˈɡrænˌfɑːðɚ/ love every grandchild/ˈɡrænˌtʃaɪld/. I also have an aunt/ænt/, /ɑːnt/, an uncle/ˈʌŋkəl/, a niece/niːs/, a nephew/ˈnɛfju/, and a cousin/ˈkʌzən/ who visits every summer. My uncle’s wife/waɪf/ and my aunt’s husband/ˈhʌzbənd/ sometimes bring their child/tʃaɪld/, a baby/ˈbeɪbi/ boy/bɔɪ/ or a little girl/ɡɝl/. In my family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/ photo there is a man/mæn/ and a woman/ˈwʊmən/, then a young/jʌŋ/ boy/bɔɪ/ and a happy/ˈhæpi/ girl/ɡɝl/. Some people/ˈpi:pəl/ look tall/tɔːl/ and thin/θɪn/; some look short/ʃɔrt/ or a bit fat/fæt/. No/noʊ/ — just a good friend for now, and one day maybe a girlfriend/ˈɡɝlfrɛnd/. Trong ảnh gia đình có đàn ông và phụ nữ, rồi cậu bé trẻ,non trẻ và cô gái hạnh phúc,vui vẻ.
