A1 Exercise · People & Family

Daily people & family blog / speaking-style passage — every new word from this level’s LanGeek lessons, with IPA, highlights, VI toggle, and TTS (IPA is display-only).

Yes/jɛs/, definitely — greetings matter every day when I talk about people/ˈpi:pəl/ and family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/. Hello/həˈloʊ/ — or just hi/haɪ/ — is how I start every conversation, and goodbye/ɡʊdˈbaɪ/ or bye/baɪ/ is how I finish. Good morning/ɡʊd ˈmɔrnɪŋ/, good afternoon/ɡʊd ˌæftərˈnuːn/, and good evening/ɡʊd ˈiːvnɪŋ/ feel polite; good night/ɡʊd ˈnaɪt/ is for the end of the day. I always say thank you/θæŋk juː/ or thanks/θæŋks/, please/pliːz/, and sorry/ˈsɑːri/ when I need to, and welcome/ˈwɛlkəm/ when someone visits. Chắc chắn rồi — lời chào quan trọng mỗi ngày khi nói về people và family. Hello — hoặc hi — là cách tôi bắt đầu, goodbye hoặc bye là cách kết thúc. Good morning, good afternoon, good evening lịch sự; good night cho cuối ngày. Tôi luôn nói thank you hoặc thanks, please, sorry khi cần, và welcome khi có khách.

If someone asks a question/ˈkwɛsʧən/, I try to give a clear answer/ˈænsɚ/: yes/jɛs/ or no/noʊ/, or OK/ˌoʊkˈeɪ/ if I’m ready/ˈrɛdi/. My name/neɪm/ is Hieu; my last name/læst neɪm/ is Nguyen. My age/eɪdʒ/ is easy to remember, and my birthday/ˈbɝːθˌdeɪ/ — or birthdate/ˈbɝːθˌdeɪt/ — is in August. My address/ˈædˌrɛs/, /əˈdrɛs/ is in the city, my phone number/foʊn ˈnʌmbɚ/ is on my phone, and I keep my passport/ˈpæˌspɔrt/ ready when I travel. I’m still single/ˈsɪŋɡəl/, not married/ˈmæˌrid/ yet. Nếu ai hỏi question, tôi cố answer rõ: yes hoặc no, hoặc OK nếu ready. Name tôi là Hieu; last name là Nguyen. Age dễ nhớ, birthday hay birthdate vào tháng Tám. Address ở thành phố, phone number trong máy, passport sẵn khi đi. Tôi vẫn single, chưa married.

If I had to describe my family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/, it would have to be this. My father/ˈfɑːðɚ/ — I also say dad/dæd/ — and my mother/ˈmʌðɚ/ — mom/mɑːm/ — are my favourite parent/ˈpɛrənt/ pair. I have one sister/ˈsɪstɚ/ and one brother/ˈbrʌðɚ/. My grandmother/ˈɡrænˌmʌðɚ/ and grandfather/ˈɡrænˌfɑːðɚ/ love every grandchild/ˈɡrænˌtʃaɪld/. I also have an aunt/ænt/, /ɑːnt/, an uncle/ˈʌŋkəl/, a niece/niːs/, a nephew/ˈnɛfju/, and a cousin/ˈkʌzən/ who visits every summer. My uncle’s wife/waɪf/ and my aunt’s husband/ˈhʌzbənd/ sometimes bring their child/tʃaɪld/, a baby/ˈbeɪbi/ boy/bɔɪ/ or a little girl/ɡɝl/. Nếu phải mô tả family, chắc chắn là thế này. Father — cũng gọi dad — và mother — mom — là parent yêu thích. Tôi có một sister và một brother. Grandmother và grandfather yêu mọi grandchild. Tôi còn aunt, uncle, niece, nephew, và cousin đến mỗi hè. Wife của chú và husband của dì đôi khi mang child — baby boy hoặc girl nhỏ.

In my family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/ photo there is a man/mæn/ and a woman/ˈwʊmən/, then a young/jʌŋ/ boy/bɔɪ/ and a happy/ˈhæpi/ girl/ɡɝl/. Some people/ˈpi:pəl/ look tall/tɔːl/ and thin/θɪn/; some look short/ʃɔrt/ or a bit fat/fæt/. My brother/ˈbrʌðɚ/ is smart/smɑːrt/ and excited/ɪkˈsaɪtɪd/ about school; when he is hungry/ˈhʌŋɡri/ or thirsty/ˈθɝːsti/ he gets angry/ˈæŋɡri/, but after food he is fine/faɪn/ and happy, never sad/sæd/ for long. My dad/dæd/ is old/oʊld/ but still strong; my mom/mɑːm/ says I’m young and ready/ˈrɛdi/ for new friends/frɛnd/. I have a boyfriend/ˈbɔɪfrɛnd/? No/noʊ/ — just a good friend for now, and one day maybe a girlfriend/ˈɡɝlfrɛnd/. Trong ảnh family có man và woman, rồi boy young và girl happy. Có people tall và thin; có người short hoặc hơi fat. Brother tôi smart và excited về trường; khi hungry hoặc thirsty thì angry, nhưng sau khi ăn thì fine và happy, không sad lâu. Dad old nhưng khỏe; mom nói tôi young và ready cho friend mới. Boyfriend? Chưa — chỉ friend, sau này có thể girlfriend.

About country/ˈkʌntri/ and nationality: I live in Vietnam, but I love stories from the United States/juːˈnaɪtɪd ˈsteɪts/ and Canada/ˈkænədə/. An American/əˈmɛrɪkən/ friend/frɛnd/, a Canadian/kəˈneɪdiən/ classmate, and a British/ˈbrɪtɪʃ/ teacher from the United Kingdom/juːˈnaɪtɪd ˈkɪŋdəm/ all help my English. I also met a German/ˈdʒɝːmən/ from Germany/ˈdʒɝːməni/, a French/frɛntʃ/ student from France/fræns/, a Spanish/ˈspænɪʃ/ tourist from Spain/speɪn/, and an Italian/ɪˈtæljən/ chef from Italy/ˈɪtəli/. Mr/ˈmɪstɚ/. Lee is an adult/əˈdʌlt/, /ˈædʌlt/; Mrs/ˈmɪsɪz/. Tran is married/ˈmæˌrid/; Miss/mɪs/ Hoa is a young/jʌŋ/ person/ˈpɝsən/ with a bright smile. Honestly, calling anyone stupid/ˈstuːpɪd/ , /ˈstjuːpɪd/ is rude — I prefer kind words for every person I meet. Về country và quốc tịch: tôi ở Vietnam, nhưng thích chuyện từ the United States và Canada. Một friend American, classmate Canadian, và teacher British từ United Kingdom giúp tiếng Anh. Tôi cũng gặp German từ Germany, student French từ France, tourist Spanish từ Spain, và chef Italian từ Italy. Mr Lee là adult; Mrs Tran married; Miss Hoa là person trẻ. Gọi ai stupid thì thô — tôi thích lời tử tế với mọi person.

So that’s my A1 speaking blog about family/ˈfæməli/, /ˈfæmli/ and greetings. A son/sʌn/ or a daughter/ˈdɔːtɚ/, a parent/ˈpɛrənt/, a friend/frɛnd/, a baby/ˈbeɪbi/ — these people/ˈpi:pəl/ make daily life feel full. When I leave, I wave goodbye/ɡʊdˈbaɪ/; when I arrive, I smile and say hello/həˈloʊ/ again. Đó là blog speaking A1 về family và chào hỏi. Son hoặc daughter, parent, friend, baby — những people này làm ngày đầy đủ. Khi đi tôi goodbye; khi tới tôi hello lần nữa.
